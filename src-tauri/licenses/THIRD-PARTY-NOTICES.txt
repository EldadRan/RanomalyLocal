FFmpeg
------
Ranomaly Local includes the FFmpeg programs "ffmpeg" and "ffprobe" (https://ffmpeg.org),
shipped as separate executables next to the application and run as separate processes.
FFmpeg is not linked into Ranomaly Local. You may replace these executables with your own
build of FFmpeg.
